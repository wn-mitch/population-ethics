# Turn 6: hybrid certificates and the bounce theorem

Frozen at the end of turn 6.

## Results

- **Hybrid certificates (D-020).** A key can rank populations first by their support's component,
  then by an axiology, so the axiology only needs to satisfy intra-component instances. This is
  sound and more general, but it certified none of the 2009 Weak Quality Addition gaps before the
  pass was stopped. Lexicographic-additive tiers cancel backgrounds, so those gaps need a
  background-sensitive axiology.
- **The bounce theorem (P13-bounce-theorem, Q-012).** Working one resistant gap by hand gave a
  proof instead of a model. Egalitarian Dominance, Quantity and Restricted Quality Addition are
  inconsistent:
  - the Quantity chain from A down to W_1 is extended by bounces, q(s) at W_1 ⪰ s at W_2 ≻ s at W_1;
  - the bounces make the bottom population arbitrarily large, which defeats the size threshold of
    Restricted Quality Addition.

  With 2009 Lemmas 2–3, {Egalitarian Dominance, GNEP, Quantity, 2009 Weak Quality Addition} is
  inconsistent. That is a 4-condition impossibility not in the catalogue, and it generalizes thesis
  Theorem 1. One instance is checked: audited, UNSAT under Z3, DPLL and the CLI, and
  principle-minimal. The proof covers every witness. The literature check is open.
- **The remaining 28 gaps.** At the lemma level with safe witnesses, several close cycles, but all
  escape under misaligned witnesses at ≤ 6 lives. No robust candidate remains.

## Lesson

A gap that resists every model class deserves a proof attempt. The resistance here came from
exactly the parameter (the size threshold m) that the bounce defeats.
