# Turn 4: the possibility map

Frozen at the end of turn 4. Turns 2 and 3 asked which contradictions exist. This turn asks the
converse: which condition sets some axiology satisfies. Together the two map the possibility
space of Arrhenius's condition language (`research/p11_possibility.py`).

## Results

- **The additive classification is exact** (`research/additive.py`, P11-additive-dichotomy).
  For V = Σ g(w), every condition reduces to constraints on g once the size quantifiers are
  eliminated in closed form. MARCO finds exactly three maximal realizable sets: drop Egalitarian
  Dominance, drop the Quality family, or drop the positivity family. It also finds 20 minimal
  impossibilities, each Egalitarian Dominance plus one Quality-type and one positivity-type
  condition. Inequality Aversion, NEP, Non-Elitism and GNEP never matter additively. This is the
  critical-level trade-off in exact form, likely known in substance.
- **Lexicographic-additive axiologies are checked exactly** (`research/lexadd.py`). A grammar of 38
  tier stacks is checked against every condition at every population size, in about a second each.
  Cross-validation:
  - the one-tier results equal the additive decision procedure;
  - counterexamples are audited instances the axiology fails.

  Negative-then-critical-level (negative welfare first, then Σ(w − c) with c between the Quality
  ranges) satisfies Egalitarian Dominance, Inequality Aversion, Non-Sadism and Quality, escaping
  Theorem 3 by violating NEP.
- **Map.** 90 minimal condition sets are realized by no checked axiology. Twenty-seven contain a
  known theorem via the recorded implications; 63 do not (Q-010). The unexplained ones are
  variants of the known theorems with one background-sensitive condition swapped out, and the
  4-set {Egalitarian Dominance, NEP, Quantity, VRC avoidance}. The census finds no contradiction
  for the leading ones at the bounds tried.

## What failed first

Bounded model checking over a finite universe is unusable as consistency evidence (D-018). It
reported total utilitarianism as avoiding the Very Repugnant Conclusion, because the violating
populations were larger than the universe.
