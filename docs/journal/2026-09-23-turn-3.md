## Results

- **The triangle's firing condition holds** (Q-007). A closed-form predicate matches the census on
  all 108 witnesses of the grid, 24 of which fire. It covers three β-type conditions, δ's base n
  and growth, and β's step and multiplier. A satisfying axiology must choose δ's n so that β's
  witness for it exceeds m.
- **Theorem 1's length law holds.** The shortest strict cycle over Quality, Quantity and
  Egalitarian Dominance has u − y + 3 edges, set by the gap between the Quality ranges. It holds in
  all 24 cases. Quantity's growth rule only sets how many lives the chain needs; below that bound
  there is no cycle.
- **With safe witnesses, the source proofs are the shortest contradictions.** With δ's n growing,
  the 3- and 4-cycles vanish from the Theorem 4, 2003 and 2009 condition sets. Only the source
  word and one adjacent-step swap remain, at length 5.
- **Repetition defeats growth** (Q-009). One 2003 survivor applies δ twice with m = 1 and closes a
  5-cycle over δ, β and Egalitarian Dominance alone. The real constraint is an exchange rate
  between δ and β, not δ's growth in m. A larger β step removes it at these bounds.
