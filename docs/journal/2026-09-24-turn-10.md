# Turn 10: an all-size finite-ladder model for Q-011's both-weakened set

Frozen at the end of turn 10. Goal: push Q-011 past the bounded SAT tables. Result: the
both-weakened variant now has an exact model at every finite population size on the ladder
`W_-1…W_6`. The source-general question stays open, as do the two singly weakened variants.

## Results

- **P21 least preorder (`research/p21_least_preorder.py`, `research/results/p21_least_preorder.json`).**
  The domain is every nonempty finite multiset over `W_-1…W_6`. The ordering is the
  reflexive/transitive closure of the primitive weak edges of Egalitarian Dominance, thesis ranged
  Non-Elitism, 2003 GNEP and 2003 VRC at witness NE `n=1`, GNEP `(u,y,n)=(5,3,1)`, VRC
  `(x,u,v,y,n,m)=(-1,4,6,3,1,1)`. Thesis Dominance Addition is an N-shaped **obligation** to verify,
  never a reverse weak edge. All four edge schemas and every witness are checked with the frozen
  ladder audit and `validate`, so this is still the reviewed source reading rather than a mutation.
- **All-size certificate.** Every edge preserves cardinality except VRC, which increases it from a
  singleton, so no cycle can contain VRC. On the cardinality-preserving edges the exact integer
  potential `Φ(P) = Σ(20t − t²)` strictly decreases: it is strictly increasing across the ladder,
  strictly concave (which handles ranged Non-Elitism), and the smallest GNEP high-to-low drop
  (`Φ(5) − Φ(3) = 24`) exceeds the largest adjacent-level gain (21). Hence the closure is a partial
  order and every Egalitarian Dominance edge stays strict. Levels are the only parameters, so the
  conclusion covers every population size.
- **Invariant and the Dominance Addition obligation.** With `I(P) = #{t ≤ 0} − #{t ≥ 5}`, every
  VRC image has `I = 1`, and ED, ranged NE and GNEP preserve `I ≥ 1` on populations of size at
  least two. The only negative-delta ranged NE cases are `(x−1, y) = (0, −1)`, where the source
  already has `I ≥ 2`, and `(4,1), (4,2), (4,3)`, where the permitted background `R(y, x)` is
  entirely positive so an `I ≥ 1` source is impossible. For Dominance Addition, `|A| ≥ 2` cannot
  reach the strictly larger target at all, and a single-life `A` can grow only through VRC
  (level `≥ u = 4`); at or above that level the threshold satisfies `x > a ≥ 4`, hence `x ≥ 5`,
  so every `B` life is at or above `W_5` and `I(B+C) ≤ −1`. The level-parameter checks for all the
  above are machine-run; the object-level path argument is recorded in the module and in Q-011.
- **Source-legal empty sub-populations are discharged, not dropped.** `corpus/readings.toml`
  leaves VRC's bag `B` and thesis DA's `C` unconstrained in size, while the ladder generators and
  `ladder.audit` require nonempty sub-populations. For a refutation that convention is harmless
  (it only drops instances), but for a model-existence claim it is not, so both families are
  treated as source instances here. With `B` empty the VRC instance is size-preserving and holds
  along the single-life ED chain `h → h−1 → … → W_-1`; with `C` empty the DA target keeps `A`'s
  size with every level above every level of `A`, so the strictly increasing potential puts it out
  of reach. An independent adversarial review of the module raised exactly these two cases and
  found both satisfied once enumerated.
- **Independent diagnostic, correctly labelled.** A bounded scan at six lives audits 206,040 source
  instances and finds no Egalitarian Dominance reversal and no forbidden Dominance Addition path.
  It is an independent source-instance check, not the proof; the certificate above carries the
  unbounded size claim.
- **No conflict with the phase-19/20 UNSAT results.** Every other fixed-witness Q-011
  construction in the repository fixes GNEP's high floor at `u = 4`, one level below this model.
  With the remaining parameters equal, their five-condition instance sets on the ladder strictly
  contain this model's, and `W_4` carries invariant weight 0 while `W_5` carries `-1` — raising
  the floor is exactly what lets the invariant and the descent potential survive. GNEP's floor is
  an existential witness the source leaves free, so both are legal; only an UNSAT **subset** of
  this model's instances could conflict with it, and that is excluded by exhibiting the order.
  The module machine-checks the strict-subset relation against the phase-18 control witness.
- **Publication.** `docs/questions.md` Q-011 records the new scope and its limits, the ledger row
  `P21-least-preorder-both-weakened` carries `checked finite-ladder theorem` with an explicit
  no-novelty stance, `corpus/literature.toml` gains collision P21 (verdict *partial*: the published
  2003 theorem and the thesis readings are the source; the finite-ladder model is not theirs), the
  `research` recipe runs the new phase, and a regression test pins the two load-bearing facts — the
  invariant's negative-delta cases and that an unrestricted background would break the ranged-NE
  step.

## Dead ends

- No source-general model: the certificate leans on the ladder being finite and on the chosen
  `u_g`, so it says nothing about welfare levels outside `W_-1…W_6`.
- The two singly weakened variants get nothing from this construction; only the both-weakened set.
- The bounded six-life scan at this witness finds no counterexample, which is exactly what the
  model predicts and therefore weak evidence on its own.
