## Verdict: partial

- **Known in substance under completeness.** Thesis Appendix B (pp. 205–206) derives Quality from
  Egalitarian Dominance and a size-restricted avoidance of the Repugnant Conclusion, given full
  comparability, so the bounce theorem then follows from Theorem 1. Verified firsthand.
- **Not found without completeness.** No work checked uses the Quantity/Egalitarian Dominance
  bounce or states Theorem 1 with a size-restricted quality condition. Every restatement keeps
  unrestricted Quality. Superiority-literature chains grow populations through Addition,
  Separability or Scaling.
- **Correction to the corollary.** Arrhenius's derivation of Restricted Quality Addition (2011
  Lemma 1.3) has an error (Thomas 2018 fn. 4). Thornley 2022 fn. 7 repairs Weak Quality Addition
  by fixing its witnesses before the background. The ladder already encodes that form, so the
  corollary holds for the repaired condition, not the source's.
