# Turn 2: Arrhenius known ground and the motif census

Frozen at the end of turn 2. Current state lives in `docs/results.md`, `docs/decisions.md` and
`docs/questions.md`; this entry records what the turn established and why.

## What was built

- **Review-first gate** (D-010, D-013). Every principle in the schemas has a reading in
  `corpus/readings.toml`. Two agents transcribed it independently from hash-verified PDFs
  (`just fetch-sources`), and both pairs agreed on all 55 statements from the 1999, 2000,
  thesis, 2003 and 2009 sources. Generation and cataloguing refuse unreviewed principles. The
  readings confirmed the v0 and 1999 encodings. MNEP adds n+1 very-low lives, which closes Q-005.
- **Thesis-family schema** (`research/ladder.py`, D-014, D-016). Twenty-eight conditions are
  encoded over an ordinal ladder of consecutive levels that includes W_0. Existentials are fixed
  by witnesses validated against the source's own constraints, and an independent audit accepts
  exactly the generated instances.
- **Complete catalogue** (`research/p8_catalogue.py`, `research/known.py`). Thesis Theorems 1–4
  and the 2003 and 2009 theorems are frozen, audited witnesses:
  - each is UNSAT without completeness under Z3, DPLL and the CLI;
  - dropping any one principle restores consistency.

  Theorem 3, Lemma 5.3 and 2009 Lemma 4 equal the 1999 skeleton at the role level. Known-ground
  coverage now counts only substantial embeddings (D-015).
- **Canonical forms and realizations** (`research/canon.py`, `research/realizations.toml`,
  D-011, D-017).
  - Exact nauty forms at L0 (shapes), L1 (roles) and L2 (lemma chains contracted).
  - Non-Elitism runs realize β, GNEP runs realize δ, and Weak Quality Addition plus δ realizes
    Restricted Quality Addition. Each realization has a solver-found example that is re-verified
    in every preorder. The GNEP realization needs W_0, which is why D-016 exists.
  - L2 recovers the 1999 skeleton from a primitive 2009 proof.
- **Census** (`research/census.py`, `research/p9_census.py`). Minimal shape cores are enumerated
  exhaustively, cycle words are searched over audited instance graphs, and the result is checked
  against MARCO.

## What the census says

1. **The motif table is two entries, and they are logic, not ethics.**
   - A minimal inconsistent set of ⪰/≻ steps is a simple cycle through a strict step. Counts
     match binary necklaces up to 6 edges.
   - With one Addition fork, it is the fork plus two strict paths into its middle argument
     (checked to 5 edges; Q-008).
   - Every thesis-family condition is ⪰ except Egalitarian Dominance. So every one of their
     minimal cores is a cycle closed by Egalitarian Dominance links, and "weak cycle closed by one
     strict link" is forced.

   The structure of these impossibility theorems lies in which cyclic words of conditions close
   on real populations, not in the shape of the proofs.
2. **Every source proof is rediscovered.**
   - Adjacent weak steps can often swap: Theorems 2 and 3 each have one extra word.
   - Theorem 1 shortens to u − y + 3 steps under its witness, versus the source's descent to
     W_1. This comes from the proof's generality, not a new theorem.
3. **The R5 triangle recurs** (Q-007). δ, β or Inequality Aversion, and Egalitarian Dominance
   close a 3-cycle whenever δ's witness does not grow fast enough with the number of negative
   lives.
   - The matcher identifies it with the 2000-family R5 triangle at the role level.
   - It appears inside the Phase 8 witness's own 2003, Theorem 4 and 2009 condition sets. Those
     frozen theorem instances stay valid instances of the source proofs, but the witness is
     refutable in three steps.
   - Both triangles say the same thing: a priority condition's witness is bounded below by
     inequality aversion and dominance.
4. **The whole family at small bounds.** Over all 28 condition forms (≤ 4 lives, ≤ 5 steps) there
   are 494 cycle words in 81 role words. Every one contains an Egalitarian Dominance step. The
   only 3-cycles are the triangle above.

## Caveats

- All census results are finite: one ladder, one witness family, bounded population size and
  cycle length. UNSAT refutes "conditions + witness" only.
- Role and realization judgments are recorded project decisions (D-009, D-017).
- The Phase 6 results in `research/results/p6_schema.json` predate the enlarged catalogue and
  D-015; their known-ground fields are stale until Phase 6 reruns.
