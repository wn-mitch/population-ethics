# Turn 5: consistency certificates

Frozen at the end of turn 5. Turn 4's possibility map left 63 condition sets that no checked
axiology realizes and no known theorem explains. This turn proves most of them consistent
without new axiologies.

## Method (D-019)

For fork-free conditions, consistency with fixed witnesses is the absence of strict cycles in the
instance graph, because the transitive closure is then an admissible quasi-ordering. Populations
are abstracted to their supports. A condition none of whose support edges lies inside a strongly
connected component is inert; if one axiology satisfies the rest exactly, the set is consistent.
A test checks the abstraction against every concrete instance on a test domain, and the six known
theorems are never certified.

## Results

- **34 of 63 gaps are certified consistent** (P12-gap-certificates), all by total utilitarianism,
  with VRC avoidance (22) or Quality (12) inert. A background-free quality condition's high
  populations receive edges only from Egalitarian Dominance among perfectly equal populations, so
  they lie on no cycle.
- **The backgrounds are load-bearing.** Thesis Theorems 3 and 4 fail with Quality in place of Weak
  Quality Addition, and the 2009 theorem fails with VRC avoidance in place of 2009 Weak Quality
  Addition. The 4-set {Egalitarian Dominance, NEP, Quantity, VRC avoidance} is consistent too.
- **29 remain open.** Twenty-six contain a background-carrying quality condition. Three are the
  2003 theorem with a weaker Dominance Addition or Non-Elitism (Q-011), the best current
  candidates for a stronger version of a known theorem.
