# Turn 11: shape obstructions and the 2003 VRC corners

The 2003 five-condition conjunction remains impossible by the published theorem. A
finite complete-preorder classification of abstract W/S/I constraints, fixed-witness
source controls, and two separate singly weakened probes sharpen the boundary. P21's
full integer-chain model is unchanged. No abstract obstruction is promoted to an
all-witness source theorem.

## Abstract shape decision (P22)

For a finite set of W/S/I constraints, branch once per I fork. A branch is feasible
exactly when the directed W/S edges plus its chosen weak fork edges contain no
strict-edge cycle; an SCC rank assignment supplies a complete preorder. The solver
returns rank certificates for feasible branches and independently replayable strict
cycles for every infeasible branch. The one-fork deletion-minimal UNSAT case necessarily
has two simple strict paths from the fork's outer arguments to its middle argument;
this is a parameterized necessity statement at arbitrary path lengths, not a claim
that any such path union is deletion-minimal. A four-edge, two-fork minimal UNSAT
control escapes the one-fork pattern. The exact branch-cycle test decides every
finite number of forks, but no small list of fixed-size obstructions or complete
parameterized multi-fork motif census is claimed.

## Source lift and literature control (P22)

For a fixed legal witness and only W/S primitive clauses, the reflexive/transitive
closure on the entire population-instance graph is a quasi-order satisfying strict
edges iff that graph has no directed cycle containing a strict edge. Its graph can
be infinite; a bounded absence of cycles proves nothing. The P8 thesis Theorem 1
and 1999 controls were independently audited as source primitive cycles and compared
at L0/L1/L2 and known-ground levels. The P18 original-2003 control replayed and
audited all 42 primitive steps; P21 is a consistency control. These do not supply
a converse for existential witnesses, N-shaped clauses, arbitrary quasi-orders, or
a claim that every abstract obstruction is realizable by source populations. The
collision verdicts for P22–P24 are partial, with checked primary sources and no
priority claim for the exact models.

## Four 2003 variants (P23)

| Non-Elitism | Dominance Addition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Unrestricted 2003 | Positive 2003 | Impossible by the published 2003 theorem; P18 separately replays one witness |
| Ranged thesis | Positive 2003 | Consistent on every finite profile over W_-1..W_k, k >= 6, also an upper-unbounded one-sided chain |
| Unrestricted 2003 | Not-worse thesis | Open; a selected legal witness is refuted by an independently audited primitive route |
| Ranged thesis | Not-worse thesis | Consistent on every finite profile over the full indexed integer chain by P21 |

The ranged-NE model is the closure of all ED, ranged NE, GNEP, VRC and positive
2003 DA edges at NE `n=1`, GNEP `(u,y,n)=(5,3,1)`, and VRC
`(x,u,v,y,n,m)=(-1,4,6,3,1,1)`. Every size-preserving non-reflexive edge
strictly descends P21's integer potential. Nonempty-bag VRC alone grows a
population; nonempty-addition DA alone shrinks one. Every VRC image has
`Psi = #{negative lives} - #{lives at W_5 or above} = 1`. ED cannot remove a
negative on this lower-bounded chain, and ranged NE and GNEP cannot decrease Psi
while a negative remains. A DA source has no negative, so no DA edge can follow
VRC. This forbids a nontrivial cycle, preserves strict ED, and proves the
all-size model, not just the finite diagnostic. The proof includes VRC's empty
low bag, DA's empty added bag, and DA with A=B empty and positive C; the
empty population is a sink. The unbounded NE/GNEP inequalities were checked
with SMT. A bounded four-life scan and audited instances are diagnostics only.

For unrestricted NE with thesis not-worse DA, the P21 witness has an audited
primitive route from (4,) and (5,) to the same DA target (6,1^7). Its N-shaped
clause conflicts with ED strictness at that witness without completeness. Another
legal witness may avoid this route; the contradiction does not settle the
source-general set. P21's ranged-background invariant fails on a concrete
unrestricted-NE instance with a negative shared background, so the P21 model
cannot simply be reused for this corner.

## Domain boundary (P24)

Restrict P21's *full-chain relation* to all finite profiles over any convex
indexed subchain of Z containing W_-1 through W_6. All source instances on the
subchain are already full-chain instances at the same legal witness;
reflexivity, transitivity, strict ED and the empty-population clauses survive
restriction. This gives arbitrarily long finite, one-sided infinite and
bi-infinite indexed domains. Recomputing primitive closure inside the subchain
would be a different construction. Separate two proposed off-chain probes:
placing a level between the old adjacent W_4 and W_5 violates consecutiveness
if old labels remain fixed, while an incomparable level lies outside the
indexed ranges but may occur in an unrestricted background. The reviewed
source does not determine a reindexing or all mixed off-chain clauses. Neither
probe was assigned a private comparability rule or used to claim a
counterexample. Q-014 records the needed independent source reading. There
is no welfare-order representation iff or all-witness forbidden-substructure
theorem.

## Verification and next question

P22's branch ranks/cycles, P23's replayed edges and path obstruction, and
P24's domain restrictions have dedicated commands and selected regression
checks; `just check` is the final project gate. The remaining mathematical
corner is unrestricted 2003 NE with thesis not-worse DA: settle it with an
all-size source-domain model at another legal witness or an every-witness
primitive contradiction. Off-chain promotion separately needs the source
fidelity in Q-014, not a stronger claim from an indexed model.
