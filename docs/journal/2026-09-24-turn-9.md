# Turn 9: Q-011 boundary, primitive control and dual-route pass

Frozen at the end of turn 9. Goal: settle Q-011's three weakened variants (the 2003 theorem with
ranged Non-Elitism and/or the thesis not-worse Dominance Addition) on route A (a source-general
contradiction) and route B (an exact model). Neither route reaches a certificate.

## Results

- **P17 source boundary (`research/p17_vrc_boundary.py`).** Five selected relata separate the two
  weakenings from the published set. The source's own Dominance Addition edge for the 2003 theorem
  is absent under the thesis form, because its positive addition is mixed (the thesis form requires
  equal added lives), and the applicable thesis clauses are N-shaped rather than reverse weak edges.
  Under ranged Non-Elitism the derived control loses its negative-background edge: the published
  Lemma 3 instance sits at levels `x+2 > 3 > 1`, outside the range that β's own background admits.
- **P18 primitive control and bounded search (`research/p18_vrc_certificate.py`).** The chosen-witness
  control re-expands 2003 Lemma 1.1/1.2 and thesis Lemma 5.2.1/5.2.2: 42 edges close UNSAT at their
  own fixed witness (β run 35 Non-Elitism edges at `n = 5`; δ run exactly four GNEP edges at
  `z = −1, m = 1`, δ's `n = 4`), dropping any of the five derived principles restores
  satisfiability, and the two-cap cycle search (caps 32 and 64, cycles up to eight edges) finds no
  cycle for any variant — recorded as partial for the two N-shaped variants, whose scan covers the
  W/S instances only. Literature verdict for the collision: **partial** — the control is
  published material, and no checked source states a strengthened 2003 theorem.
- **P19 dual-route pass (`research/p19_vrc_universal.py` plus five component modules).**
  - *Reading (D-022).* Thesis Condition β quantifies over `D ⊂ R(z, y+1)` with a closed lower
    endpoint, so ranged Non-Elitism **does** supply a negative background when β's own low level is
    negative. The phase-17/18 "lost edge" is a level-choice artefact, not a property of the
    weakening. The phase replays both directions and a test pins the pair.
  - *Mechanics.* With the repair, the written chain ED, δ, β, VRC closes UNSAT under reflexivity and
    the cycle's own transitivity for **both** Dominance Addition forms, with completeness unused.
    The not-worse thesis form does not block the closure: the weak chain entails `P2 ⪰ M1`, so
    `¬(P2 ≻ M1)` forces `M1 ⪰ P2`, contradicting the strict Egalitarian Dominance link. Dropping the
    Dominance Addition link restores satisfiability.
  - *Obstruction.* Arbitrary source-order witnesses do not force the repaired chain. Either δ's high
    population nests inside β's A level, giving the unsatisfiable
    `n_β = n_δ + n_v ≥ n_δ ≥ m_δ > m_β > n_β` (proved UNSAT over the integers, and SAT without the
    nesting equation), or δ's A lives fall in β's permitted background, requiring the bound
    `x_δ ≤ y_β + 1 ≤ y_v + 1` that no source condition supplies. 88 repaired templates show the same
    count tension in `research/p19_impossibility_ranged_ne.py`; bounded scans in
    `research/p19_impossibility_not_worse_da.py` (96-population focus, ≤ 8 lives, ≤ 6 edges) find no
    cycle or contradiction for any variant.
  - *Models.* Six count-sensitive total preorders fail one requirement each: critical-level and
    exponential additive orders fail VRC avoidance through arbitrary-size `B`; level-count
    lexicographic fails Non-Elitism for every pair and every witness `n`; count-first and thresholded
    orders fail Egalitarian Dominance or Non-Elitism. Three genuinely background-sensitive
    non-additive relations (`research/p19_vrc_model_background.py`) satisfy some source conditions for
    all finite sizes on the ladder but each fails another with an audited instance, so none models the
    five-condition set. A first-tier coefficient proof
    (`research/p19_vrc_translation_invariant.py`) excludes every translation-invariant,
    lexicographic-linear order on `W_-1 … W_6`: DA forces positive coefficients nonnegative, VRC's
    unbounded low bag zeros levels 1–3, NE zeros 4–6, ED and GNEP zero the neutral and negative
    levels. The necessary system is UNSAT and turns SAT when VRC's unbounded-bag premise is removed.
    This control locates the coefficient contradiction, but is not a model or a consistency result;
    the exclusion is ladder-relative.
- **Verdicts.** The original variant is settled externally by the published 2003 theorem; this
  turn's contribution there is the chain mechanics and the primitive control, not an independent
  proof. All three weakenings stay **open** on both routes. Q-011's "Settles it" now asks for a
  background-dependent model, since no translation-invariant order can supply one on this ladder.
- **Process.** `AGENTS.md` gains the candidate-and-independent-certificate discipline (freeze the
  reading before searching, separate discovery from promotion, stress-test outside the discovery
  sample, record finite-ladder and fixed-witness claims separately). The `research` recipe now lists
  `p17_vrc_boundary`, `p18_vrc_certificate` and `p19_impossibility_ranged_ne`, which all write result
  files but were missing from it, so `just research` can regenerate every result artifact again.

## Dead ends

- The repaired family closes only at derived witnesses; no source-order witness forces it, and the
  obstruction is exactly the nesting identity rather than a source condition.
- No cycle for any weakened variant in the bounded scans (≤ 8 lives, ≤ 6 edges).
- The count-sensitive order classes each isolate one requirement, so no member of the six-candidate
  table can be a model; the background-sensitive rerun trades that for a different single failure.
- The P18 primitive control is a re-expansion of published lemmas, so it is a diagnostic, not new
  evidence — the collision verdict is partial for that reason.
