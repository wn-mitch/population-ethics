# Turn 8: closing possibility-map gaps

Frozen at the end of turn 8. Goal: close the 28 open gaps of Q-010, each with a consistency
certificate or a proof, then make the classification ladder-independent. The first half is
mostly done; the second has not started.

## Results

- **Deep-negative tiers (D-021).** The battery gains first tiers that make only levels at or
  below W_k lexically bad. `below--2-then-total` certifies every gap that has NEP rather than
  GNEP. P11 now checks 57 axiologies: 58 minimal unrealized sets, 32 explained, 26 unexplained.
  P12 certifies 18 and leaves 8 open. Controls: no known theorem is certified.
- **P14: Theorem 3 with GNEP for NEP.** {Egalitarian Dominance, Inequality Aversion, GNEP,
  Weak Non-Sadism, Weak Quality Addition} is inconsistent, in the thesis form or the 2009 form,
  for every witness. The proof raises each negative life to W_3 with GNEP one level at a time.
  It needs neither Condition δ nor 2009 Lemma 3. `cycle` builds the proof for any witness; a
  test audits it over a grid on two ladders. Frozen instances (8 and 12 steps) are UNSAT
  without completeness, CLI-verified, and need every principle. Literature not yet checked.
- **Remaining 8 (Q-010).** All contain GNEP and a Dominance Addition form.
  - Five have a written proof that needs one level above max(W_u, Weak Quality Addition's very
    high level) (Q-013). On a finite ladder, a GNEP witness at the top level escapes it.
  - Three with VRC avoidance remain Q-011.

## Dead ends

- R(1, 1) as a very-low range: `validate` requires R(1, y) to span three levels.
- The bounded lemma sweep (≤ 6 lives) found no cycle for the P14 sets. The cycles need
  N + M > 12 lives.
- Separable saturating models (linear high levels, capped low levels) satisfy 2009 Weak Quality
  Addition and GNEP but break Inequality Aversion. Inequality Aversion forces an equal low
  population to outweigh any number of high lives, given enough lower lives.
